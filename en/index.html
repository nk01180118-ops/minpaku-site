Concept

A house that feels
like coming home.

A small two-storey house with a bright yellow front, tucked into a quiet residential street in Yao, Osaka.

Dark timber beams, wooden ceilings and soft light through paper screens keep the feel of an old Japanese home, while the beds, bathroom and kitchen are clean and comfortable.

Once you step through the front door, the whole house is yours. Relax with family or friends as if it were your own home.

Living & Dining

A place for everyone,
behind the paper screens.

Watch a movie from the red sofa, or plan tomorrow's sightseeing around the big table. The living and dining rooms, divided by sliding paper screens, make a comfortable base for families and groups. Wi-Fi is available, and the TV can play Netflix and YouTube (sign in with your own account).

つくる

tsukuruto make

Kitchen

Cook with local ingredients,
like a local.

A cherry blossom mural brings a touch of spring to the kitchen. There is a gas stove, fridge, microwave and electric kettle, plus pots, pans and enough tableware for five. Pick up something from the local supermarket and cook your own Japanese meal.
